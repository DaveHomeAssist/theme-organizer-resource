# Chapter Structure (Story Mode)

## Overview

Story Mode tells a 12-chapter narrative about inheriting and growing a raised bed garden over 3 in-game years. Each chapter = 1 season.

**Source**: `src/ui/chapter-text.js`

## Narrative Arc

**Year 1**: Learning the basics. You inherit a garden, make mistakes, discover what grows.

**Year 2**: Building confidence. Strategies get deeper, events get harder, NPCs reveal backstory.

**Year 3**: Achieving mastery and passing it on. Recipe completion, legacy items, emotional conclusion.

## How Chapters Drive Mechanics

- Crops unlock progressively by chapter
- Event deck filters by chapter availability
- Cutscenes trigger on chapter boundaries
- Difficulty curves through event severity and grid complexity
- Winter chapters include special review screens with soil analysis
